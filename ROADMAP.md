# Roadmap

## v0.1 (now)
- Safety pipeline: escalation rules, handover lock, consent, redaction, data rights, under-18 pathway
- Nigerian English and Pidgin (draft)
- Unit tests and terminal demo

## v0.2: clinical validation
- Clinical review and sign-off of all rules by licensed pharmacists and clinicians
- Native-speaker review of Pidgin wording and trigger phrases
- Evaluation against a set of anonymised, labelled test conversations (miss rate, false-alarm rate) published with each release
- Configurable referral directory (emergency, GBV, mental health, youth-friendly clinics)

## v0.3: more languages and channels
- Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo
- Ready-made adapters for Telegram and WhatsApp
- Optional local language-model classifier as a second check after the keyword rules (rules always stay first and cannot be overridden)

## v1.0: ready for others to adopt
- Encrypted database store with retention limits and audit log
- Pharmacist and safeguarding handover dashboard
- Deployment guide for NGOs, clinics and health ministries
- Submission to the Digital Public Goods Alliance registry
